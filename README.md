## 🌱 About Me

I'm a **Computer Science & Engineering student at Bikaner Technical University**, graduating in **2027**, with a strong interest in building intelligent, useful, and human-centered technology.

I enjoy working across the layers of a product — from **DSA and algorithms**, to **machine learning and data**, to **APIs, databases, backend systems, and deployment**.

What interests me most is not simply making software work, but asking:

> **Who is this system serving, how does it affect people, and can it be designed better?**

My work sits at the intersection of:

- 🧠 **Machine Learning & Applied AI**
- 📊 **Data Analysis & Experimentation**
- 🧩 **DSA & Problem Solving**
- 🏗️ **Backend & API Development**
- 🗄️ **Databases & System Design**
- 🌍 **Social-impact technology**
- 🧑‍💻 **HCI, ethics & human-centered product design**

I particularly enjoy building **text-first, friction-aware systems** that encourage intentional interaction rather than maximizing attention.

# 🚀 Featured Projects

## 🕊️ Parivaar — Collective Memory for Social Safety

**Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — The World Is One Family**

A minimalist, text-first social archive where people document experiences of **social injustice and harm**, turning lived experiences into prevention insights — so what happened to one should not happen to another.

**Why I built it:** To explore how technology can preserve lived experiences without turning sensitive stories into engagement-driven content.

**Highlights**
- Anonymous & secure experience sharing
- Prevention nuggets derived from real stories
- Inclusive of gender, caste, workplace & LGBTQ+ issues
- Designed for awareness and prevention rather than virality
- Human-centered and friction-aware interaction design

🔗 **Live:** https://sanidhya2506.github.io/Parivaar/  
💻 **GitHub:** https://github.com/sanidhya2506/Parivaar

# 🔬 Research & Social Impact

I'm particularly interested in projects where **AI and software engineering meet real human problems**.

### NeuroPath AI

**Adaptive Diagnostic Triage & Resource-Aware Prioritization Engine for Early Alzheimer's Disease**

A capstone / challenge-oriented concept exploring how intelligent systems can optimize diagnostic screening sequences under real-world resource constraints.

The core idea:

**Cognitive Tests → Blood Biomarkers → Selective MRI → PET**

The system considers factors such as diagnostic value, cost and resource availability rather than blindly sending every patient through the same expensive pathway.

# 💭 My Engineering Philosophy

> **Build systems that respect humans — not exploit them.**

I don't want to build technology simply because it can be built.

I want to understand:

**Why does this system exist?**  
**Who benefits from it?**  
**Who could be harmed by it?**  
**What friction should we remove — and what friction should we intentionally keep?**

For me, good engineering is not only about **scale, speed and accuracy**.

It's also about **intent, responsibility and human impact.**
